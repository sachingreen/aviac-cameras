SWIR lens

A 25 mm f/1.4 C-mount lens with a SWIR anti-reflection coating, focused and locked at the factory: nothing to knock loose in flight.

  • Lens25 mm f/1.4
  • Coating0.9–1.7 µm
2 / 4 · BASEBOARD + IMAGE SENSORS

InGaAs sensor

An InGaAs sensor in a ceramic package, with a heat spreader behind it for its cooler.

  • SensorInGaAs, 640 × 512
  • Pixel5 µm
3 / 4 · PROCESSING BOARD

SWIR pipeline

An FPGA board corrects pixel non-uniformity, syncs capture and sends the stream out over USB-C or Ethernet.

  • OutputUSB-C or Ethernet
4 / 4 · EXTERNALS

Box body and ports

A graphite box body with a black anodised front plate and rear rim, the Aviac mark on both sides. At the back: power and data on one M12 PoE GigE cable, a locking USB-C for setup and a microSD slot that keeps frames if the link drops.

  • Body54 × 54 × 60 mm
  • PortsPoE GigE (M12 X-coded) · USB-C · microSD
  • LensC-mount, fixed focus
