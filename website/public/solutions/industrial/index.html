Moisture, leaks and haze: seen in SWIR.

Water absorbs strongly in the short-wave infrared, so wet insulation, ponding and leaks read dark, and haze that hides a site in daylight mostly drops away. SWIRL brings that to roofs, pipe racks and plants.

What SWIR shows

Water you cannot see in daylight.

Moisture under the membrane, standing water and a leak from a pipe joint barely show in a hazy colour image. In SWIR they are dark and sharp.

Where SWIRL sees.

Short-wave infrared, 900 to 1700 nm. Water absorbs hard at 1450 nm, so wet material reads dark.
