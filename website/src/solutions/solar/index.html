How it works.

One connected service cycle, from the flight to a signed-off report.

Schedule

Pick the site and the purpose; the job records the aircraft, sensor and conditions.

Fly

TH captures the rows in thermal, every frame time-stamped and geotagged.

Review

Candidates are flagged; a qualified reviewer confirms or rejects each one.

Repair

Confirmed findings become repair work orders with evidence.

Verify

A post-repair flight checks the fix.

Report

A versioned report goes to the site owner for acceptance.
